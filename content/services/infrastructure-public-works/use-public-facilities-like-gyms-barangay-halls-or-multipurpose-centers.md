# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls, or Multipurpose Centers — Puerto Princesa City

Puerto Princesa's public facilities — barangay covered courts/gyms, multipurpose centers, and event venues — can be reserved by residents and organizations for community events, sports, and gatherings.

---

## 1) Barangay Facilities (Covered Courts, Multipurpose Halls)

**How to reserve:**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** and ask about the reservation process for the barangay gym/covered court or multipurpose center.
2. Provide the purpose, date, and expected number of attendees.
3. Some barangays charge a minimal usage or cleaning fee, especially for private events (weddings, parties).

---

## 2) City-Level Venues

For larger city-managed venues (e.g., city gyms or event centers used for city-wide activities), inquire with the **City Government** directly, as availability and booking procedures depend on the specific venue and any city events already scheduled.

---

## 3) Priorities & Restrictions

- Government and barangay-led activities (assemblies, feeding programs, disaster response use) generally take priority over private bookings.
- Facilities may be closed to private reservations during declared emergencies, as they often serve as **evacuation centers**.
