# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems — Puerto Princesa City

Water supply in Puerto Princesa is provided through a mix of the city's water district/utility and barangay-level water systems (Level I/II systems in areas without direct utility connection).

---

## 1) City Water Utility Connection

For areas covered by the city's water utility, applying for a new connection typically requires:

- Valid ID and proof of residency/ownership at the connection address
- Barangay Clearance
- Application form and connection fee (per the utility's current rates)

Inquire directly with your local water service provider for the exact process and current rates in your area.

---

## 2) Barangay-Level Water Systems

In barangays without direct utility coverage, water is often supplied through **Level I (point source, e.g., communal wells/pumps)** or **Level II (communal faucet system)** setups managed by the barangay or a Barangay Waterworks and Sanitation Association (BWSA).

**How to request access:**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** and ask about the local water system and how to connect or access a communal source.
2. Some barangay systems require a small membership or maintenance fee.

---

## 3) Reporting Water Supply Problems

- Report leaks, low pressure, or outages to your barangay or the servicing water utility.
- For barangay-managed systems, report to the BWSA or Barangay Hall directly.
