# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits — Puerto Princesa City

The **Office of the City Building Official (OCBO)** handles zoning clearances, building permits, and certificates of occupancy for Puerto Princesa City. OCBO was created by merging the former Zoning Division (previously under the City Planning and Development Office) with the Building and Public Safety Section (previously under the City Engineer's Office).

---

## 1) Zoning Clearance

**What it is:** Confirms that your intended land use (residential, commercial, industrial, etc.) is allowed under the city's **Zoning Ordinance** for that location.

**When you need it:** Before applying for a building permit, business permit for a new location, or subdividing/developing land.

---

## 2) Building Permit

**What it is:** Required under the **National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096)** before constructing, renovating, or demolishing any structure.

**Typical requirements:**

- Zoning Clearance
- Lot title/tax declaration or proof of right to use the land
- Building plans signed by a licensed architect/engineer
- Barangay Clearance
- Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (for applicable structures)

**Processing:** OCBO has streamlined processing — simple residential structures have been processed in as little as **5 business days**, with zoning, fire, and barangay fees payable at a single point.

---

## 3) Certificate of Occupancy

Issued after construction is completed and inspected, confirming the structure is safe to occupy.

---

## How to Apply

1. Secure a **Zoning Clearance** first — confirm your intended land use is allowed at that location.
2. Prepare building plans and documentary requirements.
3. Submit your application at the **Office of the City Building Official**.
4. Pay the assessed fees (zoning, building, fire, barangay — processed together).
5. Claim your **Building Permit**; apply for the **Certificate of Occupancy** once construction is complete.
