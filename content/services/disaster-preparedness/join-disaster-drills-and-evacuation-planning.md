# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)**, together with barangay disaster committees and schools, conducts regular drills under the national Disaster Risk Reduction and Management framework (RA 10121).

---

## 1) Types of Drills

- **Earthquake drills** (e.g., the nationwide "Shake Drill") — duck, cover, and hold, followed by evacuation
- **Fire drills** — in schools, offices, and barangays
- **Flood/typhoon evacuation simulations** — for flood-prone and coastal barangays

---

## 2) Barangay Evacuation Planning

Each barangay is expected to maintain an **evacuation plan** identifying:

- Designated evacuation centers (schools, barangay halls, covered courts)
- Evacuation routes for flood- or landslide-prone areas
- Contact persons and barangay disaster committee members

Ask your **Barangay Hall** for your barangay's evacuation plan and the nearest designated evacuation center.

---

## How to Join

1. Watch for drill schedules announced by **CDRRMO**, your school, workplace, or barangay.
2. Participate fully — treat every drill as if it were a real emergency.
3. Know your barangay's **evacuation center** and route in advance, before an actual disaster happens.
