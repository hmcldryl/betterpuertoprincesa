# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** coordinates emergency response for Puerto Princesa City, working with barangays, the **City Health Office**, **Fire (BFP)**, and **Police (PNP)**.

---

## 1) Emergency Response Chain

1. **Report** the emergency to the nearest hotline (911, Police, Fire, CDRRMO, or Hospital — see contacts below) or your Barangay Hall.
2. **First responders** (barangay tanod, BFP, PNP, or CDRRMO teams) are dispatched based on the nature of the emergency.
3. For disasters affecting multiple households, the **barangay disaster committee** activates the local response and evacuation plan.

---

## 2) Evacuation Routes

- Evacuation routes vary by barangay based on flood, landslide, and storm surge risk.
- Ask your **Barangay Hall** for the official evacuation route and nearest evacuation center for your area.
- During a declared emergency, follow instructions from barangay officials and CDRRMO — routes may be adjusted based on real-time conditions (e.g., a flooded road).

## How to Prepare

1. Save the hotlines above in your phone.
2. Know your barangay's evacuation center and route.
3. Prepare a **"go bag"**: ID, cash, medicines, water, flashlight, and a change of clothes.
4. Follow official updates from the **City Information Office** and your barangay during emergencies — avoid unverified social media reports.
