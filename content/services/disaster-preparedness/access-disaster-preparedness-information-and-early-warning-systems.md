# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems — Puerto Princesa City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** monitors hazards and issues advisories for Puerto Princesa City, drawing on national warning systems from PAGASA (weather) and PHIVOLCS (earthquakes/volcanoes).

---

## 1) Where Warnings Come From

- **PAGASA** — typhoon tracks, rainfall advisories, and flood warnings
- **PHIVOLCS** — earthquake information and volcanic activity advisories
- **CDRRMO** — localized advisories, barangay-level flood/landslide alerts, and evacuation orders specific to Puerto Princesa

---

## 2) How to Stay Informed

1. Follow the **City Government of Puerto Princesa** and **City Information Office** official channels for local advisories.
2. Follow **PAGASA** for typhoon and rainfall bulletins that affect Palawan.
3. Check with your **Barangay Hall** — barangays often relay warnings through loudspeakers, text brigades, or Facebook pages during an approaching storm.
4. This portal's homepage shows real-time weather for Puerto Princesa — useful as a quick check, not a replacement for official PAGASA bulletins during a storm.

---

## 3) Rainy Season / Typhoon Season Tips

- Keep your **go bag** ready (ID, cash, medicines, water, flashlight) from June to December, the Philippines' typical wet/typhoon season.
- Know your **flood/landslide risk** — ask your barangay if your area has a history of flooding.
- Charge phones and power banks ahead of an approaching storm.
