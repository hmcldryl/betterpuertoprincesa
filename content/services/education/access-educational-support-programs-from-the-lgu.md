# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Puerto Princesa City

Below are **city-led** or **LGU-facilitated** programs you can use for study support: free school supplies, library access, student employment (SPES) via PESO, scholarships, and cash/education assistance. Links point to official pages or recent announcements.

---

## 1) Free School Supplies (SEF-funded)

**What it is**

- The City provides **free school supplies** to public school learners using the **Special Education Fund (SEF)**, announced each school year with distribution schedules.

**How to access**

1. Watch your school's advisory and the **DepEd Schools Division Office of Puerto Princesa City** memos for **distribution schedules**.
2. Learners (with parent/guardian) claim supplies at the school on the announced date.

**Links**

- DepEd Puerto Princesa: [depedpuertoprincesa.ph](https://depedpuertoprincesa.ph) — division issuances and SEF distribution schedules
- News: local coverage of city school-supply distribution — [Palawan News](https://palawan-news.com)
- City info posts often echo the distribution dates via DepEd/City channels.

## 2) City Library (Free Study Space, Wi-Fi, Computers & Printing)

**What it is**

- The **Puerto Princesa City Library** offers **free Wi-Fi (including Starlink), computer use, and printing** for students; open **daily until 10:00 p.m.**, including weekends and holidays.
- Located at Mendoza Park, Rizal Avenue, Puerto Princesa City.

**How to access**

1. Go to the **Puerto Princesa City Library** during operating hours.
2. Sign the logbook; follow usage limits (to give everyone a turn).

**Links**

- [City Library Instagram (@ppc_library)](https://www.instagram.com/ppc_library/)
- News: **City Library now has Starlink Wi-Fi, open until 10 p.m.** — [Palawan News](https://palawan-news.com/city-library-now-has-starlink-wifi-and-its-open-until-10-p-m-every-day/)
- **Tel:** (048) 433-6177

## 3) Student Employment (SPES) via City PESO

**What it is**

- The **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)** lets poor but deserving **students/OSYs (15–30)** work during breaks to earn and gain experience.
- Implemented locally through the **City Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** with DOLE. Puerto Princesa's PESO has been recognized by DOLE as a top-performing PESO for SPES implementation.

**How to access**

1. Follow **City PESO of Puerto Princesa** on Facebook for **SPES application windows** and job fair advisories.
2. Prepare IDs, school docs, and income/indigency proofs per announcement.
3. Submit at the PESO office (Room 202, 2nd Floor, Old City Hall Building) or online if available; attend orientation when shortlisted.

**Links**

- [City PESO of Puerto Princesa Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/p/City-Peso-of-Puerto-Princesa-100064794756222/) (official announcements)
- DepEd/BLSS-YFD: SPES guidelines & timelines
- City press: PESO career fair / youth employment events — City Information Office

## 4) LGU-linked Education Assistance & Scholarships

**What it is**

- The City Government runs the **Mapagkalingang Programa (Caring Program)**, institutionalized under Mayor Lucilo Bayron, which provides **cash assistance to students** among other sectors (senior citizens, PWDs, barangay tanods, teaching and non-teaching staff).
- The **Office of the Representative, Palawan's 3rd District** (covering Puerto Princesa and Aborlan) and the **Palawan Provincial Government** also periodically open scholarship or educational-assistance calls for local students, separate from school-based grants.

**How to access**

1. Monitor the **City Government of Puerto Princesa / City Information Office** and the **Office of the Mayor** for official **calls** on the Mapagkalingang Programa.
2. Prepare residency and academic documents; submit by deadline; attend screening/orientation.

**Links (examples & history)**

- [City Government of Puerto Princesa – Mayor's Initiatives](https://puertoprincesa.ph/may/)
- Provincial: **YAKAP Sa Edukasyon Scholarship Program** (Palawan Provincial Government) — partnered with Palawan State University, Western Philippines University, Holy Trinity University, and Palawan Polytechnic College
- Also check school-based options in-city (e.g., **PSU scholarships**, **WPU scholarships**) which can complement LGU aid.

## 5) Educational / Cash Assistance (LGU-facilitated with DSWD)

**What it is**

- **Educational assistance pay-outs** (e.g., AICS) are nationally run by DSWD but are often **coordinated locally** with the **City** for venue and beneficiary management.

**How to access**

1. Watch announcements from **DSWD Field Office MIMAROPA** and the **City Information Office** for **Puerto Princesa payout schedules**.
2. Bring student ID, enrollment proof, and required forms on your assigned date.

**Links (context)**

- [Palawan News](https://palawan-news.com) / [Palawan Daily News](https://palawandailynews.com): local coverage of DSWD education assistance payouts

## Tips to Maximize Support

- **Stack benefits** where allowed: e.g., SEF school supplies **+** SPES stipend **+** school-based scholarship.
- Keep a folder with **PSA Birth Cert**, **Barangay Residency**, **School ID**, **Report Cards/COE**, and **2x2 photos** ready.
- Turn on notifications for **PESO** and **City Information** pages so you don't miss application windows.
